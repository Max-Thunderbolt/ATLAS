# Fake Artist Game

A mobile-friendly web app that mimics the party game "A Fake Artist Goes to New York".

## What You Need

- 4+ pens and paper (you already have these!)
- This app running on a mobile device

## How to Use

1. **Open the file**: Transfer `fake-artist.html` to your phone and open it in any browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, etc.)
   - You can email it to yourself, use AirDrop, or any file transfer method
   - Or host it on GitHub Pages and access via URL

2. **Set up the game**:
   - Enter the number of players (minimum 3)
   - Tap "Start Game"

3. **Pass the device**:
   - Each player takes a turn viewing their role privately
   - Player 1 taps "Tap to See Your Role"
   - They see if they're the fake artist or a real artist
   - Real artists see the theme and subject to draw
   - The fake artist only sees the theme
   - Tap "Done - Hide My Role" and pass to the next player

4. **Play the game**:
   - Take turns drawing on your shared paper
   - Each person adds one or two lines per turn
   - Try to identify who the fake artist is!

## Game Rules (Quick Reference)

- One player is secretly the **fake artist** who only knows the theme
- All other players know the **theme** and **subject** to draw
- Everyone takes turns adding to a shared drawing
- After drawing, vote on who you think is the fake artist
- If the fake artist is caught, they can still win by correctly guessing the subject

## Features

- 🎲 Random imposter selection
- 🎨 10 themes with 10 subjects each (100 combinations)
- 📱 Mobile-optimized interface
- 🔒 Private role reveals
- 🎮 Easy game reset

## Example Themes

- Animals, Food, Transportation
- Nature, Objects, Buildings
- Sports, People, Clothing, Music
